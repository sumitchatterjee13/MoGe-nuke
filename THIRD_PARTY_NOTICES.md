The `third_party/MoGe` directory is an unmodified copy of the `moge` package,
its licence, README, changelog and `pyproject.toml` from the upstream
repository, so the daemon can be installed without a separate clone. Training
configs, example images and assets are omitted. Model weights are downloaded
separately from https://huggingface.co/Ruicheng/moge-3-vitg and are subject to
the licence published there.

MoGe pulls in FlexGEMM (MIT), utils3d-moge (MIT) and pipeline (MIT) at install
time from their GitHub repositories.
